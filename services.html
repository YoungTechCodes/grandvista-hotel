USEFUL INFORMATION

Frequently Asked Questions.

Find helpful information about the hospitality experiences presented on this page. For specific availability and operating details, please make an enquiry.

Ask Us A Question →
What services are included in the GrandVista concept? +

The concept covers accommodation enquiries, restaurant and dining information, wellness, leisure facilities, events and guest support. Specific services and their availability require confirmation directly with the hotel.

Can I enquire about accommodation and room features? +

Yes. Visit the Rooms & Suites page to explore the accommodation collection. You can also open an individual room details page and use its enquiry links to request further information.

How can I ask about restaurant arrangements? +

Visit the Restaurant page to explore its dining concept, then use the contact page to send a question about available menus and arrangements.

Are wellness and leisure facilities available? +

The page presents wellness and leisure as part of the GrandVista hospitality concept. Contact the hotel to verify which facilities are actually available and whether any arrangements are required.

Can I make an enquiry about a private event? +

You can use the contact page to describe your event requirements and request information about possible spaces, arrangements and availability.

Does submitting an enquiry confirm a booking? +

No. An enquiry is a request for information. A reservation, event arrangement or facility booking is not confirmed until the hotel provides direct confirmation.
