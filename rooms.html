What accommodation types are available? +

The collection features deluxe rooms, executive suites, premium suites, family suites, presidential suites and garden-view room concepts.

Can I enquire about a particular room? +

Yes. Open the room details page for your preferred accommodation concept, then use its enquiry options to submit your request.

Are room prices displayed on this page? +

Room rates are not listed here. Contact the hotel to request current pricing, available dates and accommodation information.

How do I confirm room availability? +

Submit an accommodation enquiry with your preferred dates and guest information. Availability and reservation arrangements must be confirmed by the hotel.

Can I request special accommodation preferences? +

You can include your room preferences in your enquiry. Any requested arrangements will need confirmation from the hotel.
