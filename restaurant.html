Frequently Asked Questions.

Find answers to common dining questions. For current menus, opening times, prices and reservation information, please contact the restaurant directly.

Ask A Dining Question →
What type of dining experience does GrandVista offer? +

The restaurant page presents breakfast, lunch, dinner, dessert and beverage concepts within an elegant hospitality environment. Actual dining options must be confirmed with the restaurant.

Can I request information about the menu? +

Yes. Use the contact page to request information about actual menu items, ingredients, dietary requirements and available meal options.

Are food prices displayed on this page? +

No. The menu highlights are illustrative concepts. Contact the restaurant for its confirmed menu, prices and availability.

Can I enquire about group dining? +

You can submit an enquiry describing the occasion, preferred date and approximate group size. Any arrangements will require direct confirmation.

How can I ask about restaurant availability? +

Contact the restaurant with your preferred date, time and number of guests to request current availability information.

Does a dining enquiry confirm a reservation? +

No. Sending an enquiry does not reserve a table. A dining reservation is only confirmed after the restaurant provides direct confirmation.
